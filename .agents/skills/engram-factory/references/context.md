# Context before authoring or execution

Discover the target repository's actual knowledge and law from AGENTS.md and its
declared sources. Verify paths, revisions and authority before using them. Do
not transplant this repository's constitution status, registry paths, toolchain
or approval records into another project. Missing context is unknown; it is not
an assessed N/A or permission to invent a rule. Recall supplements actual source.

## Framing

Read the owner's words, current product behavior, declared project instructions
and relevant product knowledge. The orchestrator holds the owner channel and
captures actual scope, outcomes, exclusions and decision authority in the brief.

## Spec author

Read the ratified brief/ledger, actual relevant source and product behavior,
applicable constitutional packs and owner decisions. Inventory affected actors,
workflows and surfaces; include failure/boundary behavior and scope evidence.
Use spec-readiness.md. Requirements cite real rule IDs or reasoned N/A in their
Rules cells; the controller derives the rule index. Do not author duplicate
frontmatter readiness/rule arrays. Resolve material missing context before ready.

## Planner

Read accepted compact spec, brief and verified evidence. Inspect relevant current
components, domain/data contracts, accepted decisions, actual verification commands
and platform constraints. Discover registries/architecture/ADRs from the target
repository; do not assume a directory or universal test runner. Use source and
applicable vendor documentation to check mechanisms, access and prerequisites.
Name real owned paths/platform objects, dependency order, proof and recovery.

## Task author and builder

Read the accepted plan, task documents, complete assigned findings, actual gate
commands and test conventions, source revision and ownership boundaries. Builders
also require exact-plan owner acceptance/Start. Use task/build templates and the
controller's accepted manifest/index. Identify disposable resources, dependencies,
completion conditions and evidence destinations before changing anything. Source
work does not authorize live platform writes or accepted-plan changes.

## Independent reviewers and approver

Read the exact frozen candidate, authoritative applicable rules, accepted intent,
all relevant prior findings/dispositions and actual current proof. Each reviewer
applies its discipline independently on the same bytes. The approver reconciles
current PASS coverage and negative evidence without granting owner Finish/merge.
Unknown access or incomplete review is ERROR, not an inferred PASS.

## E2E QA

Read accepted plan scenarios, build handoff, exact source/environment snapshot,
scoped acceptance/rule obligations and allowed disposable resources. Follow
references/e2e-phase.md and references/roles/qa.md. Verify actual access/authority before
execution. Missing prerequisites are BLOCKED/NOT_RUN. Do not fix source, accepted
contracts, baselines or thresholds while testing.
