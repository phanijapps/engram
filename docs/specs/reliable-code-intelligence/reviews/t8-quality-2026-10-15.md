## Behavioral assessment

Admission rejects bounded wire input before tokenization/inference, checks complete embedding/scorer query contracts, and excludes nonlocal scorers before their query/code inspectors receive input. Missing/failed scorer behavior is explicit through the configured degrade/error policy. Disabled fast mode invokes no scorer. Successful ranking keeps only complete finite score batches; malformed results discard every rerank score/view and preserve full pre-score RRF ordering without importing cross-encoder thresholds.

Scorer view preparation is lazy and work-bounded by the original segment pool and configured pair cap. Exact range continuations retain original embedding identities while separately reporting successful rerank ranges/context/coverage. One-parent-first selection and stable maximum-view aggregation reuse the existing pure helpers. Pair preparation preserves UTF-8 boundaries and whole context facts; no prefix-truncated body is represented as complete. Tests reconstruct actual pair inputs from source ranges and facts rather than asserting incidental helper calls alone.

Calibration binding includes effective settings, actual input/model/tokenizer identities, query/passage representation and scorer-view contract. Artifact seals, schema version and development digest are checked; stale artifacts, operator overrides, failed scoring and cross-strategy reuse cannot manufacture positive status. Thresholding precedes diversity and output caps. Without matching calibration, inspection candidates remain explicitly insufficient. Tests use synthetic fitted artifacts only to exercise this contract; they do not claim development calibration quality.

Current authority is checked before inference and again before/after assembly, with revision fences around exact resolved parent/segment comparisons. A change discards old scores and retries once; a second change returns no inherited references/scores. Real scanner/store fixtures and mutation hooks exercise revocation and repeated revision changes. Supporting/generated sources never consume primary scoring slots or satisfy the positive gate.

Assembly keeps whole winning segment/pair records, counts repeated emitted context as repeated budget use, constrains schema array/field bounds, marks partial ranges and omissions, and re-evaluates evidence status after token/byte pruning. The oversized-winning-view regression now deliberately assigns its maximum to the large view; the earlier equal-score short tail would legitimately fit and was not a production failure. The two isolated assembly tests cover whole-record serialized-cap pruning and removal of every primary. Their isolated level is appropriate for unusually large metadata without introducing unrelated scanner/model work.
