`store_knowledge`/semantic batches MUST distinguish source-derived memory facts from graph entities/relationships, expose `BestEffort` per-step outcomes and retain succeeded steps after failure. Sourced revisions MUST use stable source identity plus a new exact-material key; an immutable retry MUST NOT be represented as a new extraction or a changed payload under the old key.
