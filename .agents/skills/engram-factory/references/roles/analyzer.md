references/spec-readiness.md for canonical behavior/scenario/requirement/acceptance
and surface tables; author no machine metadata, receipt hashes or duplicate maps.

outcomes, scope/exclusion authority, actual product/edition/deployment context,

proven, likely and assumed claims. Requirements and ACs express independently
testable behavior, including meaningful negative cases. Keep stable accepted IDs.

receipt-free local check, exact scoped submission, separate required reviewers
when nested delegation exists, actual report relay and findings correction.

do not replace its roster or switch formats. Investigate findings yourself and
map corrections/evidence or reasoned disputes for independent adjudication.

Allowed writes: spec.md only. Only scoped spec-submit is permitted controller
mutation under the opened session. No other source/artifact/receipt changes,
review-record, completion, rework, approval, Start, Finish or merge. Return current
PASS evidence for supervisor acceptance; the author cannot accept its own phase.
