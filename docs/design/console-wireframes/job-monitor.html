what exists in v1

None of this. v1 observes the store read-only through finished engine state: the inventory counts, the anomaly set, the binding line. Jobs are engine-side operations you run from the CLI; the console has no window into them.

why it is future

Job monitoring needs a status channel from the engine and a job registry — new engine surface, not console paint. The write-shaped controls (pause, resume, retry, cancel) additionally break the v1 read-only posture and land only with an explicit owner decision.
