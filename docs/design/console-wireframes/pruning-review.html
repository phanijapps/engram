what exists in v1

The anomaly panel — the same signals, surfaced read-only with drill-down links. No queue, no decisions, no history of decisions.

what the future adds

An evidence-first review flow: candidates carry their evidence, approvals state their blast radius, destructive confirmations default to cancel, and every decision is recorded with who decided and when.

why it is future

Deletion is the one operation the console must never get wrong in silence. It needs an engine write path with audit, a review-decision store, and an owner decision that the read-only posture should lift at all.
