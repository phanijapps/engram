Unified recall MUST obtain the facts lane through `MemoryService::retrieve`, other retrieval candidates through `RetrievalIndex` and beliefs through their repository, then reuse `ReciprocalRankFusion` and `compose_context`; no new lane or transport may substitute its own ranking/composer or preempt the final shared budget.
